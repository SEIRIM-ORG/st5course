## Sample Attack Tones and Triggers

Read through these examples of social engineering attack concepts to become familiar with the emotional triggers baked into these kinds of prompts:

**Urgency**
“Please approve this before 3 PM or the account may be locked.”

**Authority**
“The CEO asked me to get this handled directly with you.”

**Fear**
“Your access may be suspended unless this is confirmed today.”

**Curiosity**
“You were mentioned in this confidential internal document.”

**Helpfulness**
“Can you quickly help me open this file? I am having trouble accessing it.”

**Trust/Familiarity**
“Following up on our earlier conversation, please review this when you can.”

**Scarcity/Opportunity**
“This offer is only available to the first few employees who respond.”

**Secrecy**
“Please keep this request confidential until the announcement is made.”

**Reciprocity**
“I helped get your request approved earlier, so I need a quick favor.”

**Social Proof**
“Several people from your team have already completed this verification.”

**Confusion/Overload**
“There was an issue with the system migration, and we need everyone to reconfirm their details.”

**MFA Fatigue**
“You may receive a login prompt shortly; please approve it so we can finish setup.”

**Vendor Impersonation**
“We recently updated our payment details and need your records changed before the next invoice.”

**Executive Pressure**
“I am between meetings and need this processed immediately.”

**Compliance Pressure**
“This is required for the audit, so please complete it before end of day.”
