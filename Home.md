## Goals

If you apply the lessens in this course and push forward with a proactive cybersecurity mindset at your organization you will achieve:

- Cybersecurity awareness in yourself and your colleagues making your group much less likely to allow incoming cyber attacks to succeed
- Resiliency in backups, alternative workflows, services and more that enable your company to keep performing key work during incidents and bounce back faster once issues are resolved
- Response plans in place so staff know what to do in advance of incidents occurring
- Technical and operational preventions that greatly lessen the likelihood and impacts of cyber incidents

Our ST5 Course aims to not only enlighten all staff to be more aware of the best practices to prevent attacks, but also to educate managers on the technical preventions and resiliency measures they should ensure are being deployed and maintained in the organization.
